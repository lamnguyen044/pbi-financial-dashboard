# Financial Dashboard (Power BI)

An end-to-end Power BI project for a CFO audience: a four-page report covering the **Income Statement with Budget variance, Financial Details by quarter and territory, the Balance Sheet, and the Cash Flow Statement**, built on a star-schema model over a general-ledger (GL) dataset.

> **Important disclosure.** The source data contains no budget. The Budget used in this report is **synthetic**, generated by a documented, reproducible script (`generate_budget.py`, fixed random seed). Every Budget and variance figure illustrates the method; none of it represents a real company's plan. The report footer says so on the Income Statement page.

## 2. Headline findings (FY2020)

Figures for Year = 2020, all territories. Currency is shown with a `$` symbol as a display convention; the source has no currency field.

- **Revenue** was 7,835,369: +37.5% vs 2019 but **10.7% below Budget** (8,770,822).
- **Net profit** was 1,289,945: about flat vs 2019 (-1.0%) and **44.6% below Budget** (2,329,328). Gross margin slipped 1.5 percentage points to 68.17%.
- **The revenue shortfall (-935,453) is concentrated in the UK** (-590,334, about 63% of the gap), followed by the USA (-372,690). This reflects a real pattern in the source data: UK sales fell 42% from 2019 (727,713) to 2020 (421,784) while every other territory grew.
- **Operating expenses** rose 53.2% vs 2019 and ran 169,064 over Budget; Commissions (-69K) and Equipment depreciation (-39K) are the largest overruns, while Staff Costs came in 36K under.
- **Cash fell 11.7%** to 4,510,732 even though net profit was positive. Operating cash flow was 821,928 (63.7% of net profit, versus 106.5% in 2019) because working capital absorbed 1.2M. Inventory days (DIO) rose from 110 to 195.

Because the Budget is synthetic, treat the variance findings as a demonstration of what the report surfaces. The actual-versus-prior-year and balance-sheet/cash-flow findings come straight from the source data.

### The synthetic Budget

**Grain:** month × territory × P&L account (same keys as `Fact_GL`). Years 2019 and 2020. 2018 has no Budget because there is no prior year to plan from.

**Method:**

```
Budget = Actual(prior year, same month, territory, account)
         × (1 + growth target)
         × (1 + scenario adjustment, if any)
         × (1 + random noise, normal, sd = 3%, fixed seed 2026)
```

- **2019 growth targets** (company-wide, by COA SubClass): Sales +50%, Cost of Sales +48%, Operating Expenses +45%, Depreciation & Amortization +10%, Interest Income +20%, Interest Expense +30%, Taxation +50%.
- **2020 growth targets:** each territory's own 2018-to-2019 growth for that SubClass, multiplied by 0.9 and capped between -10% and +80%. Where no trend exists, the company-wide 2020 target is used.
- **Not budgeted (Budget = 0):** Gain/Loss on Sales of Asset, Exchange Loss/Gain, Dividend Income, because they are one-off items. Their actuals therefore appear as favourable variances under Non-operating.
- **Planted scenarios** (so the variance pages have a story): Germany 2020 Operating Expenses budget set 15% tighter; Australia Q2 2020 Sales target 20% higher; USA Q4 2019 Sales target 8% lower.
- **Sign convention** follows the GL (see below), so no sign flipping is needed.
- **Reproduce:** `python generate_budget.py --input findata.xlsx --out Fact_Budget.csv` (requires `pandas`, `numpy`, `openpyxl`). The output is deterministic.

### Sign convention (applies to the whole report)

GL amounts keep their natural sign: **income positive, expenses negative; assets, liabilities and equity positive.** As a result, **Actual minus Budget is greater than zero whenever the result is favourable**, for both revenue and cost lines. Costs show in brackets, and green/orange colours follow this rule throughout.

## 6. Data model

### Star schema

```
                     Dim_Date                Dim_Account              Dim_Territory
                  (DAX calculated)          (COA sheet)              (Territory sheet)
                         │ 1                      │ 1                        │ 1
              ┌──────────┴──────────┬─────────────┴────────────┬─────────────┘
              │ *                   │ *                        │ *
           Fact_GL              Fact_Budget         (each fact relates to all three dimensions)
        (GL, daily)         (CSV, monthly)

   Disconnected helper tables (no relationships, read through measures only):
   Dim_CashFlowLine · View_Select · PL_Layout · BS_Layout · CF_Layout       _Measures (holds all measures)
```

### Tables

| Table | Source | Grain | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Fact_GL` | `GL` sheet | One row per GL line (daily) | Columns: LineID (new index), Date, Territory_key, Account_key, Details, Amount. `Details` is kept for drill-through. |
| `Fact_Budget` | `Fact_Budget.csv` | Month × territory × P&L account | Synthetic. Dated on the first of each month; meaningful only at month level or above. |
| `Dim_Date` | DAX calculated table | One row per day, 2018-01-01 to 2020-12-31 | Range is derived from `Fact_GL` min/max. Marked as the Date table. `Month` sorted by `MonthNo`. |
| `Dim_Account` | `COA` sheet | One row per account (54) | Hierarchy Report > Class > SubClass > SubClass2 > Account > SubAccount. |
| `Dim_Territory` | `Territory` sheet | One row per country (7) | Hierarchy Region > Country. |
| `Dim_CashFlowLine` | `CashFlow_St` sheet | One row per mapping line (66) | Disconnected. Extra columns `Section Order` and `Section` (Opening cash, Operating, Investing, Financing, Closing cash). |
| `View_Select` | `DATATABLE` | 4 rows | Actual, vs Budget, vs Last Year, % of Revenue (single-select slicer on page 2). |
| `PL_Layout` | `DATATABLE` | 14 rows | Income-statement lines as ranges of `Account_key`. |
| `BS_Layout` | `DATATABLE` | 23 rows | Balance-sheet lines as ranges of `Account_key`. |
| `CF_Layout` | `DATATABLE` | 19 rows | Cash-flow lines as ranges of `Dim_CashFlowLine[Rank]`. |
| `_Measures` | Empty table | n/a | Holds every measure, grouped in display folders. |

### Relationships

Six relationships, all **one-to-many, single direction** (dimension filters fact):

| From (one side) | To (many side) |
|---|---|
| `Dim_Date[Date]` | `Fact_GL[Date]` |
| `Dim_Date[Date]` | `Fact_Budget[Date]` |
| `Dim_Account[Account_key]` | `Fact_GL[Account_key]` |
| `Dim_Account[Account_key]` | `Fact_Budget[Account_key]` |
| `Dim_Territory[Territory_key]` | `Fact_GL[Territory_key]` |
| `Dim_Territory[Territory_key]` | `Fact_Budget[Territory_key]` |

### Design decisions

- **Facts never relate to each other.** Actual and Budget meet only through the shared dimensions, so slicing by year, account or territory is consistent for both.
- **`Dim_CashFlowLine` is deliberately disconnected.** One account maps to several cash-flow lines (for example the same account feeds both "Purchase" and "Sale" of non-current assets), so a relationship would be many-to-many and ambiguous. The `CF Amount` measure reads it through `TREATAS` instead.
- **Layout tables instead of hierarchies for the statements.** A matrix grouped by COA Class cannot show Net Revenue, Gross Profit or EBIT, because those lines do not exist in the chart of accounts. Each layout table row defines a statement line as a key range, with a `Kind` column (`Header`, `Detail`, `Subtotal`, `Total`) used for formatting and a `Line Display` column that indents detail rows and upper-cases totals. Trade-off: statement rows do not expand to account level; account detail lives in the operating-expenses-by-account chart (and the planned drill-through page).
- **Single measure per statement** (`PL View`, `BS Value`, `CF Value`) with the layout row selected through `SELECTEDVALUE`, so one measure serves every column (quarter, year, comparison mode).
- **Date table is derived from the data**, so extending the GL extends the calendar with no manual edit.

**Dim_Date definition:**

```dax
Dim_Date =
VAR _start = DATE ( YEAR ( MIN ( Fact_GL[Date] ) ), 1, 1 )
VAR _end   = DATE ( YEAR ( MAX ( Fact_GL[Date] ) ), 12, 31 )
RETURN
ADDCOLUMNS (
    CALENDAR ( _start, _end ),
    "Year", YEAR ( [Date] ),
    "QuarterNo", QUARTER ( [Date] ),
    "Quarter", "Q" & QUARTER ( [Date] ),
    "MonthNo", MONTH ( [Date] ),
    "Month", FORMAT ( [Date], "MMM" ),
    "MonthStart", DATE ( YEAR ( [Date] ), MONTH ( [Date] ), 1 ),
    "YearMonth", FORMAT ( [Date], "YYYY-MM" ),
    "YearQuarter", YEAR ( [Date] ) & "-Q" & QUARTER ( [Date] ),
    "DayOfMonth", DAY ( [Date] ),
    "DayName", FORMAT ( [Date], "ddd" ),
    "DayOfWeekNo", WEEKDAY ( [Date], 2 ),
    "IsMonthEnd", [Date] = EOMONTH ( [Date], 0 )
)
```

## 7. Measures

All measures live in `_Measures` and are in `dax/measures.dax` (also reproduced in the `Measures` sheet of `CFO_Model_and_Budget.xlsx`). Nothing is hard-coded in the visuals.

### Core pattern

```dax
GL Amount = SUM ( Fact_GL[Amount] )

Actual   = CALCULATE ( [GL Amount], KEEPFILTERS ( Dim_Account[Report] = "Profit and Loss" ) )
Budget   = CALCULATE ( SUM ( Fact_Budget[Amount] ), KEEPFILTERS ( Dim_Account[Report] = "Profit and Loss" ) )
Variance = [Actual] - [Budget]
Variance % = DIVIDE ( [Variance], ABS ( [Budget] ) )
```

**Why `KEEPFILTERS`:** a plain filter inside `CALCULATE` would *replace* any filter a visual already puts on `Report`, so a Balance Sheet row in a visual would suddenly show P&L amounts. `KEEPFILTERS` *intersects* instead, so a visual grouped by Class shows only P&L accounts, and the Adjusting account never leaks in. The denominator of `Variance %` uses `ABS` so a negative Budget (a cost line) still gives the right direction.

| **Base** | `GL Amount`, `Actual`, `Budget`, `Variance`, `Variance %` |

### Definitions worth knowing

- **P&L lines** are filtered by COA class: Revenue = SubClass "Sales" (Sales plus Sales Return); Gross Profit = Class "Trading account"; Operating Expenses = Class "Operating account" (includes depreciation and amortization); **EBIT = Trading account + Operating account**; Net Profit = all P&L accounts. Non-operating income, interest expense and tax sit *below* EBIT, as on the statement.

  ```dax
  EBIT = CALCULATE ( [Actual], Dim_Account[Class] IN { "Trading account", "Operating account" } )
  ```

- **Statement lines (layout tables).** `PL Actual` selects a key range from the current layout row and sums over it:

  ```dax
  PL Actual =
  VAR _lo = SELECTEDVALUE ( PL_Layout[KeyFrom] )
  VAR _hi = SELECTEDVALUE ( PL_Layout[KeyTo] )
  RETURN
      CALCULATE ( [Actual],
          FILTER ( ALL ( Dim_Account[Account_key] ),
                   Dim_Account[Account_key] >= _lo && Dim_Account[Account_key] <= _hi ) )
  ```

  `PL View` then switches among Actual, Variance, change vs last year and % of revenue depending on the selected button, and uses a **dynamic format string** so the same measure shows amounts or percentages.

- **Balance sheet is semi-additive.** `Closing Balance` is the cumulative GL balance up to the last date in the current filter, so a year column shows the 31 December balance, not a sum of months:

  ```dax
  Closing Balance =
  VAR _end = MAX ( Dim_Date[Date] )
  RETURN
      CALCULATE ( [GL Amount],
          KEEPFILTERS ( Dim_Account[Report] = "Balance Sheet" ),
          REMOVEFILTERS ( Dim_Date ),
          Dim_Date[Date] <= _end )
  ```

  The GL starts on 2018-01-01 with no opening balances, so opening cash for 2018 is 0.

- **Working-capital days** (`DSO`, `DIO`, `DPO`) = closing balance ÷ period revenue (or COGS) × days in the selected period. They use closing balances, not averages.

- **Cash flow (indirect method).** `CF Amount` iterates `Dim_CashFlowLine` and, for each mapping row, reads the GL for that `Account_key` using `TREATAS`, applying the row's `ValueType`:

  | ValueType | Meaning |
  |---|---|
  | `Opening_balance` / `Closing_balance` | Balance before the period starts / at period end |
  | `All_FTP` | Net movement in the period |
  | `All_FTP_Positive` / `All_FTP_Negative` | Only GL rows greater than / less than zero |
  | suffix `_CS` | Change sign (balance-sheet increase = cash outflow) |

  Opening plus net change equals closing for every year and territory, which is the basis of the `Check Cash Flow` measure.

- **Prior-year measures guard against missing history.** For 2018 (no prior year) the PY and "vs PY" measures return blank instead of echoing the Actual.

## 10. Refresh guide

**What is loaded:** `findata.xlsx` (sheets GL, COA, Territory, CashFlow_St) and `Fact_Budget.csv`. Parameterise the two file paths in Power Query (for example `SourceFolder`) so the report can move between machines.

**To refresh:**

1. Replace or update the source files in the source folder.
2. If the GL changed, regenerate the Budget: `python generate_budget.py --input findata.xlsx --out Fact_Budget.csv`.
3. In Power BI Desktop choose **Home > Refresh**.
4. Open pages 3 and 4 and confirm both Check badges are green.
5. Spot-check the appendix figures for any period that did not change.

**What updates automatically:** `Dim_Date` (range follows `Fact_GL`), all measures, all visuals.

**What does *not* update automatically** (static tables; edit them if the chart of accounts changes):

- `PL_Layout`, `BS_Layout` and `CF_Layout` hold account-key and rank ranges. If account keys are added or renumbered, update the ranges so subtotals stay complete. The check badges will flag an imbalance.
- `View_Select` (four fixed labels).

**Scheduled refresh in the Power BI Service** is not configured in this project. A published version would need the files in OneDrive/SharePoint or an on-premises gateway.

## 11. Assumptions and known limitations

1. **Budget is synthetic** (section 4). Variance findings illustrate the report, not a real plan.
2. **No currency in the source.** The `$` symbol is a display convention only.
3. **Closing balances start from zero on 2018-01-01.** There is no pre-2018 history, so 2018 opening cash is 0 and 2018 cash-flow lines show the full build-up.
4. **2018 has no Budget and no prior year.** Comparisons are blank for 2018. Do not use Year = All for comparisons: Actual would span three years and Budget two.
5. **Working-capital days use closing balances** and the days in the selected period; they are meaningful for one year at a time.
6. **Non-operating variance looks large** (for example +256% in 2020) because gains, exchange items and dividend income are deliberately not budgeted.
7. **Cost-line YoY and variance signs.** Costs are negative, so a cost increase shows as a negative YoY percentage. The report footnote explains this.
8. **"Equipment" and "Amortization of Intangible Assets" are depreciation and amortization accounts** that sit inside the Operating class. They appear in the operating-expenses-by-account chart so its total matches Operating Expenses on page 1.
9. **Waterfall charts** colour the first bar as an increase (Power BI limitation); Total bars are navy.
10. **Layout tables are static.** Statement lines do not auto-expand to account level.
11. **Account-level drill-through is not yet built** (see roadmap).
12. **The source data is a sample ledger**, not a real company. No personal or confidential data is included.
